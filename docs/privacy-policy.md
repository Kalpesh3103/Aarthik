# Aarthik Privacy Policy

Last updated: **17 September 2026**

**Developer:** thermodevworks  
**Contact:** thermodevworks@gmail.com  

## 1. About this policy

Aarthik is a personal finance tracker. This policy explains how it handles information entered manually or imported from bank SMS and payment screenshots. No Aarthik user account is required.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The app stores accounts, bank names, optional account suffixes, balances, credit limits, transactions, amounts, dates, categories, notes, payment references, transfers, preferences, and extracted import drafts in app-private storage. SMS import records also contain sender details and message fingerprints for duplicate detection. Aarthik does not connect to your bank or request bank login credentials.

## 3. Optional SMS access

With your consent and Android SMS permission, the app receives incoming SMS in the background, even when the app is closed. It checks sender information and parses supported bank transaction messages to automatically save income and expenses. It does not scan your existing inbox. Unsupported and unreadable messages are ignored. Raw SMS bodies are not persisted or logged by the app. Extracted transaction information and duplicate-detection records are retained locally. Turn import off in **More → SMS settings** or revoke SMS permission in Android app settings.

## 4. Screenshot import and Google ML Kit

When you share a payment screenshot, the app uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition to process that image on the device. The app retains extracted draft fields, not a copy of the screenshot or raw OCR text. The original image remains with its source app.

ML Kit may send SDK diagnostics and usage metrics, including device/app information and identifiers, to Google under its SDK practices. On-device image processing does not imply that the SDK sends no telemetry. See [ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Notifications

Optional system notifications show imported transaction amounts and provide access to review. Android 13 and later require notification permission. Denying it does not stop SMS transaction saving. You can manage notifications and lock-screen visibility in Android settings.

## 6. Storage, backup, and sharing

Aarthik has no developer-operated server for your financial records and does not upload SMS bodies, screenshots, or financial records to such a server. The app does not provide financial records, SMS contents, or screenshots to advertising SDKs.

### Advertising and consent

Home and More display Google AdMob banner ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Ad processing depends on consent, region, and Google's configuration. See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Google's User Messaging Platform processes consent information and displays privacy messages where required. Ads are requested only when it permits requests. Where available, reopen **More → Privacy choices for ads** to review your choices. Android settings also provide advertising privacy controls. Manual financial tracking remains available without an internet connection. Google's retention and deletion practices apply to data processed by its services; clearing local app records does not delete that data.

Android backup is enabled: app data may be included in cloud backup or device transfer depending on your system settings, Android version, and device provider. thermodevworks does not access or control these system backups.

Contacting the developer by email shares whatever you choose to include with the developer and email providers. Avoid sending sensitive financial details.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until removed. You can delete transactions and discard screenshot drafts in the app. Account deletion is restricted when related records exist; archiving is not deletion. Dismissing SMS review retains saved transactions and import history used to prevent duplicates.

To remove all local app records, clear Aarthik's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. These actions do not necessarily remove system backups, your original SMS, or source screenshots. Manage those separately using the relevant system or source-app controls; a backup restore may restore app records.

## 8. Security and your choices

The app uses Android app-private storage and permission controls. This is not a promise of additional database encryption or absolute security. Protect your phone with a screen lock. Manual tracking works without SMS or notification permission. System settings control permissions and backup behavior.

## 9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior changes. The date above identifies this version. For privacy questions, contact thermodevworks at **thermodevworks@gmail.com**.
